# Make A Mark Impact Drive Context

This file defines product language for the current prototype and planned hackathon R1. It is subordinate to the [inclusive R1 concept](docs/Make-a-Mark-R1-inclusive-AI-concept.md) for product requirements.

## Product concepts

**R1 prototype:** The hackathon build with one fictional freight mission, a manually advanced live-style demo view, a limited source-reviewed evidence library, and an optional Race Engineer. It does not connect to AMF1 systems.

**Mission scenario:** A fictional, deterministic freight situation with route choices and game feedback. Its values are not AMF1 operations, telemetry, or measured impact.

**Simulated live view:** A user-advanced local fixture with simulated timestamps, signal definitions, units, and feed-state examples. It is not a live AMF1 feed.

**Reported impact:** A source-backed claim from a published report with a source, reporting period, location where available, and limitations. Do not call it AMF1-approved unless AMF1 has actually approved it.

**Illustrative impact placeholder:** A fictional sample value demonstrating a future interface. It must carry “Illustrative demo data — not live AMF1 data or a measured impact result.”

**Source-reviewed evidence:** A public claim that has been checked against its source for inclusion in the prototype. This wording does not imply formal AMF1 endorsement.

**Prepared answer:** A reviewed prewritten response used when the hosted model is unconfigured or unavailable. Label it as prepared, not AI-generated.

**Race Engineer:** An optional, server-mediated explanation tool that responds only to user questions and points back to reviewed records or clearly labeled demo context.

**Discovery:** A locally stored interaction such as completing the mission or opening a record. It is not an impact measure, user profile, or proof of learning.

## Trust and interaction rules

- Keep the four categories — simulated live view, reported impact, illustrative placeholder, and mission scenario — visually distinct.
- Let people choose the mission, evidence library, or answer detail they want; do not infer ability or preferences from age.
- Support keyboard and touch operation, reduced motion, and an untimed non-driving route.
- Do not require sign-in or persist player/question history on the server.
- Keep the interface usable without the provider. Unsupported evidence leads to a limitation or no-answer response, never a guess.
