# Cognizant Hackathon Presentation

**Make A Mark: Impact Drive · About 3 minutes**

- **[Show home screen]** We’re presenting Make A Mark: Impact Drive, an inclusive F1 fan experience that helps people **play, understand, and verify**.
- F1 fans see the racing, but less of the operational choices behind each race weekend, such as moving a team and its equipment between events.
- Sustainability information can be difficult to connect to that story and check against its source. We’re exploring a more engaging way to make the context understandable and the evidence inspectable.
- **[Show freight mission]** Fans choose between fictional freight routes and see a deterministic outcome. It introduces the trade-off through play.
- The mission is fictional: it is not an actual AMF1 decision, does not calculate emissions, and does not claim that a player’s choice changes real-world impact.
- **[Show simulated live view]** The values and timestamps come from a fixed local demo sequence, not a live AMF1 feed. It demonstrates how an interface can show changing, delayed, or unavailable data without presenting sample values as measured impact.
- **[Open evidence library and a reported record]** Fans can explore a small set of source-reviewed claims from the published Make A Mark ESG report, with reporting periods, source locations, and limitations.
- Reported claims stay separate from illustrative examples. A target, a demo placeholder, and a reported result should not look interchangeable.
- **[Open Race Engineer]** The planned Race Engineer would explain terms and report claims, cite relevant reviewed records, and say when the evidence is insufficient. It would respond only when a fan asks.
- In the current prototype, Race Engineer responses are prepared; live model integration and evidence retrieval are not connected yet.
- **[Show accessibility controls or discovery recap]** Fans can start with the mission or go directly to evidence, use keyboard or touch, reduce motion, and explore without an account. A local recap records discoveries without building a player profile.
- **[Close on home screen]** Our hackathon goal is a trustworthy fan activation: make the story engaging, the evidence checkable, and simulated data clear.
- A future AMF1- or event-sponsored version could remain free for fans, but the buyer, funding, and demand still need validation. This is not an approved AMF1 product, and it does not use internal AMF1 data.
- Impact Drive helps fans connect the race they watch with the choices behind it—and gives them a clear path from curiosity to evidence.
- Thank you.
