# 2025 Make A Mark evidence: source review

Reviewed 2026-10-01 for the 2025 evidence candidates covering Environment, Belong, and Community. “Source-reviewed” means checked against the official public source for accurate display in this prototype; it does not mean AMF1 endorsed the app or independently assured every claim.

## Source publication

- **Publisher:** Aston Martin Aramco Formula One™ Team (AMF1)
- **Title / edition:** *2025 Make A Mark Report* (the official page labels it “2025 REPORT” and “2025 MAKE A MARK REPORT”)
- **Publication date:** No exact publication date is stated on the official page. “2025” is the report/results label, not a verified publication date.
- **Reporting period:** The official page presents the metrics below in its 2025 report section and describes 2025 results/cohort/programme. It does not state a separate overall start/end date on the page. Preserve 2025 as the stated results year; do not infer a fiscal period.
- **Official report landing page:** [Make A Mark | Sustainability and Impact](https://www.astonmartinf1.com/en-GB/make-a-mark)
- **Linked report PDF:** [2025 Make A Mark Report (PDF)](https://downloads.astonmartinf1.com/MakeAMark_ESG_Report_2025.pdf) (download link from the official landing page).
- **Review limitation:** The linked 15.7 MB PDF was downloaded and text-extracted for review on 2026-10-01. The six claims and report locations below were checked in the report itself, including relevant footnotes. The PDF identifies the displayed printed page numbers; its download-page anchors use the corresponding zero-offset PDF page index.

## Candidate claims

### ENV-2025-TRAVEL-LOGISTICS-REDUCTION — Environment

- **Claim type:** Reported emissions reduction.
- **Exact official claim text:** “In addition to strengthening our net zero pathway, we achieved a 14 per cent reduction in travel and logistics emissions through smarter logistics planning and investment in Sustainable Aviation Fuel.”
- **Value / unit:** 14 per cent reduction.
- **Reporting period:** Presented under the 2025 report section; the page describes progress in 2025. It does not state the comparison baseline or exact measurement dates for this reduction.
- **Report location:** “Net Zero by 2050” section (official report landing page, lines 117–127 in the page text); printed p. 9 (PDF p. 8); additional air freight methodology at printed p. 24 (PDF p. 23), footnote 14.
- **Source:** [Official Make A Mark page](https://www.astonmartinf1.com/en-GB/make-a-mark#net-zero-by-2050) · [linked report PDF](https://downloads.astonmartinf1.com/MakeAMark_ESG_Report_2025.pdf)
- **Review note and limits:** Attribute the reduction and stated drivers to AMF1. The accessible source text does not identify the comparator year/baseline, calculation boundary, absolute emissions values, or each intervention’s contribution. Do not imply a 14 per cent reduction in all team emissions or in Scope 3 overall.

### ENV-2025-TRAVEL-LOGISTICS-AVOIDED — Environment

- **Claim type:** Reported avoided emissions.
- **Exact official claim text:** “A total of 1,188 tCO₂e were avoided, which is equivalent to 88,153 laps around Silverstone.”
- **Value / unit:** 1,188 tCO₂e avoided; the page also gives an equivalence of 88,153 Silverstone laps.
- **Reporting period:** Presented alongside the 2025 travel and logistics reduction under the 2025 report section. The page does not give exact measurement dates.
- **Report location:** “Net Zero by 2050” section, immediately after the 14 per cent travel/logistics reduction statement (official page text, lines 117–127); printed p. 9 (PDF p. 8), footnote 2; methodology also printed p. 24 (PDF p. 23), footnote 14.
- **Source:** [Official Make A Mark page](https://www.astonmartinf1.com/en-GB/make-a-mark#net-zero-by-2050) · [linked report PDF](https://downloads.astonmartinf1.com/MakeAMark_ESG_Report_2025.pdf)
- **Review note and limits:** Keep “avoided” and tCO₂e exactly; do not recast it as a reduction in measured inventory emissions. The accessible page does not state the counterfactual, calculation method, emissions factors, or the derivation of the Silverstone-lap equivalence. The 1,188 tCO₂e statement appears in the same paragraph as travel/logistics, but the precise accounting boundary needs the PDF methodology before making a more specific attribution.

### BEL-2025-ALETO-NETWORK — Belong

- **Claim type:** Self-reported participant outcome.
- **Exact official claim text:** “Upon completion of the programme, 93 per cent of the 2025 cohort felt they grew their professional network and 90 per cent felt they grew their leadership skills, public speaking and confidence.”
- **Value / unit:** 93 per cent of the 2025 cohort.
- **Reporting period:** 2025 Aleto cohort, after programme completion. The page says the third programme year welcomed 15 university students for mentorship over nine months.
- **Report location:** “Aleto Leadership Programme” section, programme cohort description and outcomes (official page text, lines 129–141); printed p. 10 (PDF p. 9), footnote 4; footnote 4 cites the 2024–2025 Aleto Foundation impact report.
- **Source:** [Official Make A Mark page](https://www.astonmartinf1.com/en-GB/make-a-mark#aleto-leadership-programme) · [linked report PDF](https://downloads.astonmartinf1.com/MakeAMark_ESG_Report_2025.pdf)
- **Review note and limits:** Preserve “felt” to identify this as participants’ reported perception, not an objectively measured network expansion. The page does not state the survey question, response count/response rate, instrument, or validation method. It gives 15 students welcomed to the programme, but does not expressly state the denominator used for the 93 per cent figure.

### BEL-2025-ALETO-SKILLS-CONFIDENCE — Belong

- **Claim type:** Self-reported participant outcome.
- **Exact official claim text:** “Upon completion of the programme, 93 per cent of the 2025 cohort felt they grew their professional network and 90 per cent felt they grew their leadership skills, public speaking and confidence.”
- **Value / unit:** 90 per cent of the 2025 cohort (as phrased in the same completion-outcome passage).
- **Reporting period:** 2025 Aleto cohort, upon completion.
- **Report location:** “Aleto Leadership Programme” section, same outcome passage as the network result (official page text, lines 135–141); printed p. 10 (PDF p. 9), footnote 4; footnote 4 cites the 2024–2025 Aleto Foundation impact report.
- **Source:** [Official Make A Mark page](https://www.astonmartinf1.com/en-GB/make-a-mark#aleto-leadership-programme) · [linked report PDF](https://downloads.astonmartinf1.com/MakeAMark_ESG_Report_2025.pdf)
- **Review note and limits:** Preserve “felt”; the source reports perceived growth, not objectively tested skill gains. It combines leadership skills, public speaking, and confidence in one percentage, so it does not support a separate percentage for each outcome. Survey wording, response count/rate, and methodology are not available in the accessible page text; the denominator is not expressly given.

### COM-2025-MAKE-A-MARK-WEEK-STUDENTS — Community

- **Claim type:** Programme reach / participation.
- **Exact official claim text:** “Our fourth annual Make A Mark Week engaged 257 students across 14 schools and community groups in STEM and career development workshops, delivered in collaboration with key partners, including Arm, Cognizant, ELEMIS and Xerox.”
- **Value / unit:** 257 students.
- **Reporting period:** Presented as the 2025 Make A Mark Week in the 2025 report section. The page says it took place ahead of the British Grand Prix but does not state event dates.
- **Report location:** “Make A Mark week” section and opening reach statement (official page text, lines 143–161); printed p. 11 (PDF p. 10).
- **Source:** [Official Make A Mark page](https://www.astonmartinf1.com/en-GB/make-a-mark#make-a-mark-week) · [linked report PDF](https://downloads.astonmartinf1.com/MakeAMark_ESG_Report_2025.pdf)
- **Review note and limits:** “Engaged” supports a reported reach/participation claim. The accessible page does not explain whether the count represents unique students, attendance instances, registration, or another counting method. Do not say the programme caused measured learning or career outcomes.

### COM-2025-MAKE-A-MARK-WEEK-ORGANISATIONS — Community

- **Claim type:** Programme reach / participating education and community organisations.
- **Exact official claim text:** The same sentence says Make A Mark Week “engaged 257 students across 14 schools and community groups”.
- **Value / unit:** 14 schools and community groups combined.
- **Reporting period:** 2025 Make A Mark Week, presented under the 2025 report section; event dates are not stated on the accessible page.
- **Report location:** “Make A Mark week” section and opening reach statement (official page text, lines 143–161); printed p. 11 (PDF p. 10).
- **Source:** [Official Make A Mark page](https://www.astonmartinf1.com/en-GB/make-a-mark#make-a-mark-week) · [linked report PDF](https://downloads.astonmartinf1.com/MakeAMark_ESG_Report_2025.pdf)
- **Review note and limits:** The source provides a combined total only. It does not give separate counts for schools versus community groups or name the 14 organisations in this sentence. Do not display “14 schools plus community groups” or infer 14 schools; the wording supports 14 across both categories.

## Candidate substantiation summary

All six proposed metric themes have direct support in the downloaded official AMF1 2025 report and on its landing page. No exact publication date, full reporting-period boundaries, Aleto survey denominator/response rate, or Make A Mark Week event dates are stated. The Aleto percentages are framed as what cohort members “felt,” and the 14-school/community-group count is combined rather than split. The report footnotes attribute the Aleto outcomes to the 2024–2025 Aleto Foundation impact report but do not provide its respondent/method details here.

For app records, preserve the team's attribution and qualifiers; identify the Aleto outcomes as self-reported; represent 14 as a combined schools/community-groups count; and do not imply that source review means AMF1 endorsement or independent assurance.
